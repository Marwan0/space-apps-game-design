Workshop Outline: Game & Simulation Design for Space Apps

Speaker: Marwan Ayyad

Duration: 2 Hours

Purpose: Comprehensive session structure for NASA Space Apps Cairo 2026

I. Introduction & The Hook (10-15 mins)

  • Speaker Intro: "Hello, I am Marwan Ayyad. I love guiding brilliant minds into the exciting world of games."
  • The Golden Rule: "A game should always be a choice, never forced."
  • Why Games for NASA Data? Discuss the purpose of games (fun, knowledge), brain impact (dopamine, resilience), and the difference between a dashboard and a true simulation.

II. The Essentials of Interaction (15-20 mins)

  • The Core Loop: Action/Choice → System Response → Feedback.
  • Variables & Constraints: Extracting state variables (e.g., mass, power) from a NASA challenge.
  • The Fidelity Ladder: Translating real science into simplified, transparent game rules.

III. Deconstructing Success: Past Winners (15 mins)

  • Henry's Farm Adventures (2025): Earth-science data placed directly inside player decisions.
  • PureFlow (2025): Choices visibly change system states using deterministic rules instead of complex AI.
  • Connected Earth Museum (2024): Teaching through exploration and pacing without traditional points.

IV. The 48-Hour Playbook: Tools & AI Strategy (20 mins)

  • Scope Traps to Avoid: No open worlds, no multiplayer, no custom 3D pipelines, and no complex realistic physics.
  • Tool Culling: Prioritize lightweight tools (Browser, Three.js, Godot, Unity) for an MVP.
  • AI as Your Copilot: Use AI for code generation (state changes), rule balancing, and fast placeholder assets.
  • Task Management: Track variables and scope using Trello, Meistertask, or Wrike.

V. Live Demonstration (15 mins)

  • From Challenge to Mechanic: Using a past challenge (e.g., 2024 Galactic Games) for a live workflow demo.
  • Live Workflow Steps: Define the goal, pick 2-4 variables, make one decision that changes the state, and show immediate feedback.

VI. Hands-On Practice: The 1-Minute MVP (30-35 mins)

  • The Challenge: Teams have 20 minutes to design a "One-Minute Core Experience".
  • Requirements: Goal + 3 variables + 2 decisions + rule table + clear end state.
  • Playtesting Swap: Teams trade prototypes, test clarity, and write one revision based on the feedback.

VII. Wrap-Up & Q&A (10 mins)

  • Defining "Done": Ship one scenario/level with core data working first. Polish and graphics come last.
  • The 5 Golden Rules: Design decisions first, integrate science into rules, ensure fast feedback, simplify science transparently, and ship one complete loop.
