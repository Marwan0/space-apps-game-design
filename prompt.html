The AI prototype loop

  1. Paste the prompt below into any AI chat and fill in your spec.
  2. Save the reply as game.html and double-click it.
  3. Play one minute: does the choice matter?
  4. Ask for one change at a time. Paste errors exactly.
  5. Keep every version that works.
